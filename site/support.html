Support

Stuck on something? Found a bug?

Jury Simulator is a single-developer project. Here are the fastest paths to a fix.

Report a bug or request a feature

Open an issue at github.com/chartmann1590/jury-simulator/issues. If it's a crash, please include your phone model and Android version — that's usually enough to reproduce.

Common questions

The app downloads 3.65 GB on first launch — is that normal?

Yes. The on-device AI model is what generates every trial, witness statement, and juror line. After the one-time download the app runs entirely offline.

Why are there ads?

Hosting the model and the bandwidth for the first-launch download cost real money. A small banner and an occasional interstitial cover that. There is no paid tier (yet).

How do I turn off personalized ads?

Open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads and either reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization. Jury Simulator will keep working — you'll just see less-relevant ads.

Will it run on iPhone?

Not yet. Jury Simulator is Android-only.

How much storage does it actually use?

About 4 GB after the model download. The model itself is 3.65 GB; the rest is the app, your case history, and your notebook.

What devices is it tested on?

Android 8.0 (API 26) is the minimum. The on-device model runs comfortably on most phones from the last four years; older or low-RAM devices will be slower between juror replies.
