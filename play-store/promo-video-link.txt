# Promo Video

The promo video lives in the repo at marketing/assets/jurysim-promo.mp4 (mirrored at site/assets/jurysim-promo.mp4).

Play Store accepts only YouTube URLs in the listing — not direct uploads.

Steps:
1. Upload marketing/assets/jurysim-promo.mp4 to YouTube as Public or Unlisted.
2. Paste the youtu.be/<id> URL into the Play Console "Promo video" field.
3. Replace this file's contents with the final URL once uploaded.

Voiceover narration script: see marketing/promo-video-voiceover.md
Voiceover audio (already produced): marketing/assets/jurysim-voiceover.wav
