What would you decide if justice depended on your vote?

Jury Simulator puts you in the jury box for AI-generated criminal trials. Move through the full process: voir dire (jury selection), opening statements, witness testimony, evidence review, closing arguments, deliberation, and final verdict. Every case is generated fresh — different defendant, different charges, different witnesses — so no two sessions are alike.

Why people play Jury Simulator:

• The full trial flow, nothing skipped — voir dire to verdict
• Eleven AI jurors, each with a profession, a personality, and a hidden bias
• Private one-on-one juror chats during deliberation
• Up to five rounds of voting — unanimous or you hang the jury
• A built-in notebook to track contradictions, evidence, and witness inconsistencies
• Case history so you can revisit decisions you regret
• Customizable juror profile

How a session goes:

1) Start a new case. Read the charges.
2) Pass voir dire — answer the judge's questions and hope the prosecution doesn't strike you.
3) Sit through the trial: opening, witnesses (you can question them), evidence review, closing.
4) Walk into the jury room. Talk to all eleven jurors at once, or pull one aside.
5) Vote. If you're not unanimous after five rounds, the judge declares a mistrial.

Runs on your device. After a one-time AI model download, every trial is generated locally — no account, no cloud, no server holding your gameplay. Your notes and votes never leave your phone.

Designed for legal-curious folks, debate enthusiasts, students of evidence and reasoning, and anyone who has wondered how juries actually decide things.

Free to play. A small banner ad and an occasional interstitial cover the hosting bill — no paywall, no subscription.
