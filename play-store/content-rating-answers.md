# Play Console — IARC Content Rating Questionnaire

Answers below correspond to the questions Google Play's IARC questionnaire asks for a Simulation/Role-Playing app. Use these when filling out the form in Play Console under **Policy → App content → Content ratings**.

## Violence
- **Does the app contain violence?** Yes — *cartoonish or text-only*.
  - Trials may reference assault, theft, or homicide as charges, presented as text-only courtroom dialogue. No depicted violence, no animated combat, no graphic imagery.
- **Realistic violence?** No.
- **Blood, gore, dismemberment?** No.

## Sexual content
- **Sexual content / nudity?** No.

## Profanity
- **Strong language?** No — the AI is constrained to courtroom-appropriate language. Mild language may appear in juror dialogue rarely.
- **Crude humor?** No.

## Drugs / alcohol / tobacco
- **References to drug use?** Possible — drug-related charges may appear in case generation as the offense being tried, presented as text references only. No glorification.
- **Alcohol references?** Possible — same as above (DUI cases, etc.).
- **Tobacco references?** No.

## Gambling
- **Real-money gambling?** No.
- **Simulated gambling?** No.

## User-generated content / social
- **Users can interact / share content?** No — fully single-player; no online multiplayer; no chat with other humans.
- **User-generated content?** No — players type input only to the in-app AI; nothing is published.
- **Shares user location?** No (the app does not access device location; AdMob may use approximate IP-based geolocation, see Data Safety).

## Mature themes
- **Crime themes?** Yes — trials cover criminal offenses (theft, assault, fraud, homicide, drug, DUI). Treated procedurally, not glorified.
- **Horror?** No.
- **Discrimination / hate?** No.

## Recommended rating outcome
- **IARC**: Teen / 12+ in most regions; PEGI 12 (mild violence references, infrequent crime themes, in-app advertising).
- **Target age**: 13+
