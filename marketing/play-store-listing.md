## Short Description (<= 80 chars)

## Full Description (<= 4000 chars)
What if you could experience jury duty from your phone?

Jury Simulator puts you in the jury box for realistic, replayable courtroom cases. You will move through the full process: jury selection (voir dire), opening statements, witness testimony, evidence review, closing arguments, deliberation, and final verdict.

Each case is generated with AI and designed to feel dynamic, so no two sessions are exactly the same.

Why players love Jury Simulator:
- Full jury-duty journey from selection to verdict
- AI-powered courtroom dialogue and juror conversations
- Deliberation with 11 distinct AI jurors, each with unique personalities
- Private one-on-one juror chats during deliberation
- Interactive trial phases with evolving evidence and testimony
- Built-in notes to track facts and arguments
- Case history to revisit past decisions and outcomes
- Custom juror profile and adjustable AI settings

How it works:
1) Start a new case
2) Complete voir dire
3) Follow testimony and evidence across trial phases
4) Deliberate with your fellow jurors
5) Vote and help decide the final verdict

Designed for:
- Legal enthusiasts
- Debate and critical-thinking fans
- Students exploring courtroom dynamics
- Anyone curious about how juries reason under uncertainty

AI and model support:
Jury Simulator supports modern LLM-powered simulation and can run with on-device or configured model options depending on your setup. Configure your preferred model in settings, then jump into immersive courtroom roleplay.

Whether you are aiming for a quick case or a deep deliberation session, Jury Simulator gives you a thoughtful, engaging way to test your reasoning, challenge your assumptions, and decide justice under pressure.

Download Jury Simulator and take your seat in the jury box.
